HOW IT WORKS

Simple Steps. Responsible Giving.

Before donating, make sure you understand the process and can verify who is managing the fund.

01

Read the Details

Review the purpose of the fund, the donation amount, and how funds will be used.

02

Donate Only If Willing

Use only a payment method officially confirmed by the fund administrator. Never share your PIN or one-time password.

03

Track the Impact

Ask for confirmation and look for updates showing how donations support people in need.

TRANSPARENCY

Before collecting donations, publish the fund's responsible organizer, eligibility criteria, fees (if any), and a regular summary of income and distributions.

✓
Recommended Public Records

Donation totals, distribution dates, categories of assistance, and remaining balance.

✓
Protect People's Privacy

Do not publish recipients' phone numbers, account details, or personal stories without consent.

✓

For actual online payments, use a reputable payment provider and never collect PINs or passwords on this website.
